🚀 Launching WorkOut Log — A Privacy-First Workout Tracker

I'm excited to share WorkOut Log, an iOS app I built to solve a problem I had: finding a simple, private workout tracker that doesn't require cloud sync or an account.

What makes it different:
• 100% offline — all data stored locally on your device
• Zero data collection — no analytics, no tracking, no third parties
• Custom exercise library — create your own exercises with categories
• Volume trends — visualize progress with daily/weekly/monthly charts
• VoiceOver support — fully accessible for all users

• Swift 6 + SwiftUI + SwiftData (iOS 18+)
• Clean Architecture with domain-driven design
• Native Apple frameworks only (no dependencies)

### App Name
- **Official:** WorkOut Log (capital W, capital O, space)
- **Short:** WorkOut Log
- **Handle:** @workoutlog (if available)

### Tagline Options
1. "Track workouts. Own your data."
2. "Your workouts. Your data. Your device."
3. "Offline-first workout tracking."
4. "Privacy-focused fitness tracking."

### Color Scheme
- **Primary:** Blue (system accent)
- **Categories:**
  - Lower Body: Blue
  - Upper Body: Green
  - Cardio: Red
  - Full Body: Orange
- **Background:** System background (adapts to light/dark mode)

### Voice & Tone
- **Direct:** No fluff, no hype
- **Honest:** Clear about limitations (no cloud sync means no cross-device)
- **Technical:** Comfortable using terms like "SwiftData," "local storage," "offline-first"
- **Inclusive:** Accessible to all fitness levels and abilities
