**Effective Date:** 2025-10-16
**Last Updated:** 2025-10-16

## Introduction

WorkOut Log ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our iOS application.

**TL;DR:** We collect **ZERO** data. Everything stays on your device.

---

## Data Collection

### We Do NOT Collect Any Data

WorkOut Log is designed with privacy-first principles:

- ❌ **No personal information** (name, email, phone, address)
- ❌ **No workout data** sent to servers
- ❌ **No usage analytics** or tracking
- ❌ **No advertising identifiers**
- ❌ **No third-party SDKs** that collect data
- ❌ **No crash reporting** to external services
- ❌ **No location tracking**
- ❌ **No camera or photo access**
- ❌ **No contacts or calendar access**

### What Data is Stored Locally

All data generated by the App is stored **exclusively on your device** using Apple's SwiftData framework:

- **Workout sessions:** Date, sets, exercises
- **Exercise library:** User-created exercise names and categories
- **Volume trends:** Calculated from your local workout history
- **App preferences:** UI state (e.g., selected trend scope, category filters)

This data is:
- ✅ **Stored only on your iPhone**
- ✅ **Encrypted by iOS** (if device encryption is enabled)
- ✅ **Backed up to iCloud** (only if you enable iCloud Backup in iOS Settings)
- ✅ **Never transmitted** over the internet
- ✅ **Deleted permanently** when you delete the app

---

## Network Connectivity

WorkOut Log **does not require internet access** and **makes zero network requests**.

The App is 100% offline and will function fully in:
- ✈️ Airplane mode
- 🏔️ Areas with no cellular signal
- 🔒 Networks that block internet access

We have **no servers, no databases, no cloud storage** that could be hacked or breached.

## Third-Party Services

WorkOut Log uses **zero third-party services**. The App is built entirely with Apple's native frameworks:

- **SwiftUI** for user interface
- **SwiftData** for local storage
- **Swift Charts** for visualizations
- **Foundation & Observation** for app logic

No external SDKs, no ad networks, no analytics platforms.

## Your Data Rights

Since we do not collect any data, there is no data to:
- Request access to
- Request deletion of
- Request correction of
- Request export of

**However,** you have full control over your local data:

### To View Your Data
All data is viewable within the App's UI (WorkLog, Trends tabs).

### To Export Your Data
Currently, the App does not have an export feature. Your data is stored locally in SwiftData format.

### To Delete Your Data
1. Delete the WorkOut Log app from your iPhone
2. All data will be permanently deleted from your device
3. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, delete the backup to remove data from iCloud

---

## Data Security

While we do not store data on servers, your local data is protected by:

- **iOS System Encryption:** All app data is encrypted by iOS when device passcode/Face ID/Touch ID is enabled
- **Sandboxing:** iOS prevents other apps from accessing WorkOut Log's data
- **No Network Exposure:** Since data never leaves your device, it cannot be intercepted

**Best Practices:**
- Enable device passcode/biometric lock
- Keep iOS updated to latest version
- Enable iCloud Backup for data recovery (optional)
