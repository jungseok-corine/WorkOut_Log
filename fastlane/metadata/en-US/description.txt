# WorkOut Log — Offline Workout Tracker

Track your workouts with complete privacy. All data stays on your device—no accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

## Key Features

### 📝 Session & Set Tracking
• Record workout sessions with date selection
• Add sets with exercise, weight (kg), and reps
• Automatic volume calculation (weight × reps)
• Persistent exercise selection for rapid data entry
• Swipe-to-delete sessions and sets

### 🏋️ Custom Exercise Library
• Create your own exercises with custom names
• Four main categories: Lower Body, Upper Body, Cardio, Full Body
• Search with instant results
• Swipe-to-delete exercises (with safety validation)
• Exercises grouped by category for easy browsing

### 📊 Workout Log
• Chronological session list (newest first)
• Category badges showing exercise types per session
• Total volume displayed per session
• Sets grouped by exercise with section headers
• Per-exercise set numbering and subtotal volumes

### 📈 Trends & Analytics
• Three viewing scopes: Daily (≤10 days), Weekly (8 weeks), Monthly (6 months)
• "Show All Days" toggle for Daily mode (include zero-volume days)
• Category filters (Lower/Upper/Cardio/Full)
• Interactive line charts with volume visualization
• At-a-glance progress tracking

### 💬 Daily Motivation
• Random workout quote on app launch
• 15 curated quotes from athletes and leaders
• Auto-dismiss after 2.5 seconds (or tap to skip)
• VoiceOver-friendly with extended duration

## Privacy & Security

### 100% Offline
• No internet required—works in airplane mode
• No cloud sync, no servers, no databases
• All data stored locally using Apple's SwiftData
• Your data never leaves your iPhone

### Zero Data Collection
• No personal information collected
• No usage analytics or tracking
• No advertising identifiers
• No third-party SDKs
• No crash reporting to external services

### Your Data, Your Control
• All workout data stored on your device only
• Encrypted by iOS (when device passcode enabled)
• Backed up to iCloud only if you enable iOS Backup
• Permanently deleted when you uninstall the app
