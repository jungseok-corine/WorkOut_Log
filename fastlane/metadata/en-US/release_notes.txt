Version 1.0.0 — Initial Release

Welcome to WorkOut Log! Track your workouts with complete privacy.

New Features:
• Session & set tracking with automatic volume calculation
• Custom exercise library with 4 categories (Lower/Upper/Cardio/Full)
• Workout log with category badges and exercise grouping
• Trends visualization (Daily/Weekly/Monthly)
• Daily motivational quotes on app launch
• 100% offline — no accounts, no cloud, no tracking

Built with Swift 6 + SwiftUI + SwiftData for iOS 18.
